Alfred

Alfred is a private, self-hosted personal assistant built and used by one person for their own household and home lab. It is not a commercial product and is not offered to the public.

With its owner's permission, Alfred reads the owner's Gmail and reads and adds events to the owner's Google Calendar, so it can answer questions like "what's on my calendar this week?" or "anything important in my email?". All processing happens on the owner's own computers.
